Sign-Language Communication for Healthcare

Bridging the Gap Between Deaf Patients and Healthcare.

Unmute is an AI-powered sign-language communication platform that enables two-way communication between Deaf patients and healthcare professionals. It translates supported sign language into text or speech, and converts clinicians' spoken or written messages into visual sign-language output — so routine care can happen directly, in the room.

What Unmute Does

The Platform

01

Sign to Speech & Text

The Patient Speaks

The Deaf patient signs. Unmute recognises supported ASL and turns it into clear text on screen and spoken words for the practitioner — so a patient can describe pain, symptoms and history in their own language, without waiting for an interpreter.
02

Speech to Sign

The Practitioner Responds

When the practitioner speaks or types, Unmute converts the message into visual sign-language output — closing the loop. Instructions, explanations and next steps reach the patient in a form they can actually understand, not through lip-reading or a handwritten note.
03

Team Plan for Hospitals

Institutional Access

A monthly institutional subscription covering up to 10 practitioners, with custom plans for larger healthcare institutions. One department, one subscription — giving every clinician on the team a tool they can use at the bedside without booking an interpreter.

For Hospitals

Reduce reliance on on-site interpreters.

On-site interpreters are scarce, expensive and rarely available the moment a patient arrives. Unmute gives practitioners an accessible tool to communicate directly with Deaf patients during routine interactions — so the everyday conversation no longer depends on scheduling.

Unmute begins with ASL and routine healthcare interactions, with more advanced translation capabilities developed over time.
