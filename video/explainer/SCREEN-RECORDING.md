# Filming the product walkthrough

I cannot record the product. It needs a camera pointed at a hand, in a real
browser, and the sandbox has no browser and no webcam. Anyone claiming to
generate that footage is generating an animation of a product, not the product.

Here is the shot list so the recording is twenty minutes, not an afternoon.

## Before you hit record

- Phone, **top-level Chrome tab** — camera is blocked in an embedded preview
- `app.liveunmute.com/live.html`
- Bright, even light behind the phone; hands against a plain wall
- Mute notifications. One banner over the camera preview wastes a take
- Screen record at 1080p, 30 fps. Vertical if it is going to TikTok or Reels

## The six shots

| Shot | Length | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 6 s | Ready screen. Camera selected. Press start. |
| 2 | 4 s | Hold a **thumb up** until the app commits it. Let the text land on screen. |
| 3 | 4 s | Hold a **number** — 2 or 3 — for the pain scale. |
| 4 | 8 s | Say a phrase from the clinician list out loud. Let the matched phrase appear. |
| 5 | 6 s | Say something **outside** the list. The refusal message appears. **Do not cut this.** |
| 6 | 4 s | The patient's answer spoken aloud. |

Shot 5 is the most valuable six seconds you have. A demo that shows only what
works reads as a demo; one that shows the refusal reads as a product.

## Editing

Cut on action, no transitions, no music under the refusal shot. Burn no
captions into the footage — they will be wrong later; publish a `.vtt` next to
it instead so it can be corrected.

If you send me the raw clips, I can cut them together with the same type system
as the explainer: title card, the six shots, end card.
