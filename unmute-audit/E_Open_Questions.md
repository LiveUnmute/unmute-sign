## Missing evidence classes (entire categories absent from Drive)

1. **Engineering artefacts** — repository, architecture, model cards, test results, benchmarks
2. **Financial documents** — budget, projections, pricing, cap table, grant applications
3. **Research records** — interview notes, consent forms, transcripts, survey results
4. **Institutional documents** — LOIs, MOUs, pilot agreements, procurement correspondence
5. **Legal documents** — incorporation, IP assignment, privacy policy, terms of use
6. **Design artefacts** — wireframes, user flows, Figma files, avatar assets
7. **Marketing execution** — published posts, landing page copy, analytics, enquiry log

The absence of category 1 and category 3 is the most consequential: the venture's claims rest on work that left no trace in the workspace.
