## The honest one-paragraph status

Unmute is an idea with an unusually good problem statement and an unusually thin evidence base. The founder has correctly identified a real, severe, and under-served problem; has done more secondary research than most pre-seed founders; and has produced a pitch package that is well ahead of the underlying work. What does not yet exist is the product, the Nigerian data, the clinical relationship, the team, the price, or the pilot. The venture is roughly at the stage of "credible thesis, zero proof" — which is a legitimate place to be, provided the decks stop implying otherwise.
